# Enhancements

Things to build into Dighouse, one line each. Add to it freely. When an item becomes a goal in a loop, link the loop. When it ships, move it to Done with the pull request.

## Seeing a find (planned in [SEE_LOOP.md](SEE_LOOP.md))

- Reasons for and against on the find card, as a count.
- Ranks A, B and C by how many methods agree.
- Four numbers on the card: height against the ground around it, ground points or forest, change between flights, finds within 200 m.
- A west–east and north–south switch on the slice, and a 1.70 m figure.
- VAT, multi-scale relief and local dominance views (RVT), each with one line saying what it shows.
- Closed hollows and ground points per m² as views.
- Flights from different years around a find, with a slider and a change map.
- A divider and a transparency slider between the ground and old maps or photos.
- A 3D close view with stretched height and a ruler.
- Find similar, from a find a person agreed.
- A person marks a spot or an area for the agent to check.
- ADAF as a second detector (needs a decision).
